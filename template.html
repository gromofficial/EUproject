13 — FAQ

QUESTIONS

Do we need to travel with you?

No. That's the point. You send us the clothes; we handle the production.

Do you only shoot during Fashion Week?

No. Fashion Week is a natural moment for the kind of content we create, but we produce shoots throughout the year.

Can you shoot both photos and video?

Yes. We can build a production around photography, Reels, or both.

Where do you shoot?

Primarily Paris and Milan, with other locations available depending on the project.

What do we need to provide?

Your clothing, your brand direction and the goals for the campaign. We take care of the production.

Can you create content specifically for Instagram?

Yes. The production can be designed around vertical video, social-first photography, Stories and Reels.
